Privacy Policy

At MetrQ, we take your privacy seriously. Our platform is designed with privacy-by-default principles:

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information beyond your chosen nickname
  • All authentication data is hashed using industry-standard bcrypt
  • Your analytical workflows remain private and are not shared with third parties
  • We do not use tracking cookies or analytics that identify individual users
  • Data retention follows strict policies with automatic purging after specified periods

For complete privacy policy details, please contact us at info@metrq.com
